# Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) Detection

This project uses a **Random Forest Classifier** to detect the likelihood of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) based on screening data. The model is trained on publicly available datasets and deployed with an interactive interface using **Streamlit**.

## 🚀 Features

- Predicts ASD likelihood using Random Forest algorithm
- Interactive UI for input and prediction (Streamlit)
- Data preprocessing and feature engineering
- Model evaluation with accuracy, confusion matrix, and classification report
- Lightweight and easy-to-deploy

## 📊 Dataset

- Source: [Autism Screening Adult Dataset – UCI Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/datasets/Autism+Screening+Adult)
- Features include age, gender, family history, score from screening questions, and more.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas, NumPy
- Scikit-learn (RandomForestClassifier)
- Matplotlib / Seaborn (for visualization)
- Streamlit (for UI)

The AQ-10 (Autism Spectrum Quotient - 10) consists of 10 questions that assess traits commonly associated with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). These questions are designed to be answered with a simple "Yes" or "No" to give an indication of whether an individual may have ASD-related traits. The questions generally focus on social interactions, communication, attention to detail, and flexibility of thinking.

Here are the types of questions you might find in the AQ-10:

1 Social Skills:

"I prefer to do things on my own rather than with others."

This question assesses the tendency toward social withdrawal or preference for solitary activities, which is common in individuals with ASD.

2 Attention to Detail:

"I tend to notice details that others do not."

Individuals with ASD may often focus more on details rather than the overall picture, and this question helps assess that tendency.

3 Communication:

"I find it hard to read between the lines when someone is talking to me."

This question measures challenges with understanding non-verbal cues or implied meanings, which is typical in ASD.

4 Imagination:

"I enjoy participating in group activities."

A difficulty in enjoying or participating in group activities could suggest challenges with social interactions, a common trait in ASD.

5 Flexibility of Thought:

"I find it hard to change my routine."

A strong preference for routines and difficulty with changes can be a characteristic of ASD, as many individuals with ASD have rigid thinking patterns.

6 Social Relationships:

"I prefer to do things in a particular way, and I get upset if things are done differently."

This question looks at how inflexible a person might be when routines or structures are altered.

7 Empathy:

"I find it hard to understand how people are feeling."

Empathy challenges, such as understanding or relating to others' emotions, are common in people with ASD.

8 Interest in Others:

"I find it difficult to make conversation."

Social communication challenges are often present in ASD, and this question addresses that aspect.

9 Sensory Sensitivity:

"I am often irritated by things like bright lights or loud noises."

Many people with ASD have heightened sensitivity to sensory inputs like sound, light, or touch.

10 Routine and Change:

"I find it hard to deal with unfamiliar situations."

This measures the difficulty individuals with ASD may experience when confronted with change or new environments.
